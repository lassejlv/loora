# The marketing pages are server-rendered and meant to be indexed. Signed-in
# surfaces are not — but they are excluded with a noindex meta tag on the route
# rather than here, because a crawler that is not allowed to fetch a page never
# reads the tag on it, and a URL blocked this way can still be indexed on the
# strength of inbound links alone. Disallow is therefore reserved for things
# that should never be fetched at all.
User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /api/
Disallow: /.well-known/
Disallow: /*?design=
Disallow: /*?d=
Disallow: /*?settings=
